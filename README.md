# Chocolate_Sales_Performance_Dashboard

## Project Overview
An interactive **Microsoft Excel dashboard** analyzing chocolate sales performance across products, countries, sales channels, and salespersons during 2022–2023.
The data was cleaned, organized, and analyzed to create meaningful KPIs, Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, and interactive visualizations.
The dashboard provides a consolidated view of overall sales performance and allows users to explore key business metrics using interactive slicers for Product, Country, and Channel. It helps identify top-performing products and markets, understand channel contribution, evaluate salesperson performance, and compare marketing expenditure with revenue.

## Objectives
* Analyze revenue and order performance
* Identify top-performing products and countries
* Compare sales channels
* Evaluate marketing spend and discounts
* Identify top-performing salespersons
* Support data-driven business decisions

## Tech Stack
* Microsoft Excel
* Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts
* Slicers

|  Total Orders      |  **199,563** |

## Dashboard Features
* Revenue by Product & Country
* Orders by Country
* Boxes Shipped by Product
* Revenue by Sales Channel
* Marketing Spend vs Revenue
* Top 10 Salespersons
* Interactive Product, Country & Channel slicers

## Key Business Insights
* **70% Dark Bar** is the top-performing product by revenue.
* **Australia** generates the highest overall revenue.
* **Retail** is the largest sales channel, contributing **52%** of revenue.
* **Arun Mehta** is the highest-performing salesperson.
* Marketing spend can be compared with revenue to identify stronger-performing markets.
* The average discount across sales is **13.08%**.
